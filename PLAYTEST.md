### 2026-11-21  (build v0.5.0 on the iPad, Robert's notes after a week of play)

**Confused him / frustrated him**
- Take away: the eaten apples were already faded out, so he just counted the bright ones and never did the sum.

**Robert's notes**
- Home screen: the Reading tile's 13 stars ran off the tile. "Looks like too many stars."
- Other kids want to play without taking over where he is.

**Changed as a result (v0.6.0)**
- Home tiles show one bar and one star, not a star per level.
- Players: each has their own levels and stars, picked by an animal face on the home screen. Added behind the gear.
- Take away: all the apples start whole. The crossing-out and counting only play after a wrong tap or the lightbulb. No "watch me first" rounds on that level.

**Changed as a result (v0.7.0, same day)**
- Robert's idea: a take-away level that comes first, where he taps the apples away himself (6 - 4: tap 4 of the 6 apples, then tap the answer). It is Numbers level 5 and the number-only take away is now level 6.
- Robert: "add more reading games and more difficult." Reading levels 14-18: riddles, opposites and same words, which one does not belong, what happens next, why.
- To watch for: does he tap the apples without being shown, and are levels 14-18 hard enough?

**Changed as a result (v0.8.0, same day)**
- Robert: "adding more vibrant interactive educational games. use higgsfield to make it more lively." Added a Balloons world (pop the right balloon), a Thinking world (sort things into two baskets), the frog on the number line (Numbers levels 7-9) and a sticker book that fills up as he plays.
- To watch for: which of the new games he goes back to, whether he opens the sticker book by himself, and whether he drags or taps in the sorting game.

**Changed as a result (v0.9.0, same day)**
- Robert: "add more games and more levels." Added memory pairs and what-comes-next patterns to Thinking (now 15 levels), four more Balloons levels (10), five more Numbers levels (14) and two more Time levels (7).
- To watch for: is 8 cards too many for the pairs game, and are the sums to 20 too big a jump after the frog?
- Robert: "maybe we should add puzzles and coloring games." v0.10.0 adds a Puzzles world (jigsaws from 2 to 16 pieces) and a Coloring world (free coloring, then color by number, by color word and by sum). To watch for: can he drag the small pieces of the 12 and 16 piece puzzles, and can he read the labels inside the small parts of the coloring pictures?
- Robert: the "Watch me first" rounds on the adding levels give the answer away. Removed from Numbers levels 1-4. The counting now only plays after a wrong tap or the lightbulb.
